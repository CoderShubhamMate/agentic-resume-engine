# 04 — AREAS OF INTEREST (LAST SECTION OF EVERY RESUME)

This section replaces the old fixed "Interests" block. It is tailored per job and is the ONLY honest place for relevant technologies the candidate does not yet have verified experience with.

## 4.1 Placement and wording
- Always the LAST section, with the heading "Areas of Interest".
- Content starts with "Interested in developing expertise in:" followed by a comma-separated list. "Currently exploring:" is allowed. "Currently learning:" ONLY if Master_Data explicitly states active learning.
- Never write "skilled in", "experienced in", "proficient in", "hands-on with" or any language implying experience.
- Compact: 1 to 2 lines. It must never push the resume past one page.

## 4.2 What goes in (3 to 8 items)
1. Technologies, tools, domains or concepts from THIS JD that are relevant to the role and NOT verified in Master_Data. Pick the most important ones. Do not dump every JD keyword.
2. If fewer than 3 qualify, fill up with genuine interests recorded in Master_Data (for example Generative AI, RAG, Cloud Architecture, Distributed Systems).
3. If it still cannot honestly reach 3, use fewer. Never pad with irrelevant keywords. Mention this in the final response.

## 4.3 What stays out
- Anything verified in Master_Data (it belongs in Skills, Experience or Projects).
- `cert_only` items already shown on the certification line.
- Technologies that are unsupported AND irrelevant to the role.
- Anything added only to manipulate ATS ranking.

## 4.4 Hard separation
- VERIFIED → Skills / Experience / Projects
- INTEREST OR LEARNING → Areas of Interest only
- UNSUPPORTED AND IRRELEVANT → Excluded

Never place an interest item inside a bullet, a project line, a skills line, a summary or a certification. Never invent project experience to justify an interest.

## 4.5 Checker behavior
Items that appear ONLY in the Areas of Interest section are not fabrication. See `07-match-and-revision-loop.md` section 7.3 for how to handle the matcher's `unverified_in_resume` list.
